A research agent for materials science.

MatAgent retrieves and reads the literature, identifies missing data, and formulates hypotheses screened by chemical-validity checks. Every run is recorded for review.

Reads the literature

MatAgent searches paper indexes, ranks the results, and answers questions from the page text, with page citations.

Finds and ranks papers

Each request becomes up to five search queries. The results are merged and ranked, and the top papers are downloaded.

Method · LR 1.1 · Semantic Scholar · Google Scholar if installed · arXiv when both return nothing or no PDF downloads · de‑duplicated by paper ID, then DOI · lexical score, no embeddings · share of query terms in title ×2, in abstract ×1 · recency ×0.1 · citations (capped at 100) ×0.05 · top 5 with score ≥ 0.5 downloaded (defaults)

Answers cite the page

Ask a question of a paper: the best-matching passages are retrieved, and a language model answers from them, citing pages as [p. N].

Method · MMR 4.1⁠–⁠4.3 · page text in overlapping chunks of ~1,200 characters · BM25, top k (6 by default, ≤ 2 per page) · opt‑in: ColSmol‑500M ranks page images by late‑interaction MaxSim and a vision‑language model reads the top pages; any failure falls back to text · a PDF with no text layer is read page by page by a vision‑language model, when one is configured · MMR 4.3 runs LR 1.1, then reads up to 3 PDFs at a time

One searchable knowledge base

Papers and their figures, mode runs, datasets, chat answers and Deep Research reports share one index, searched by keyword and, when embeddings are available, by meaning. Figure descriptions are written by a vision-language model and labelled model-generated.

Method · SQLite · FTS5 BM25 · sqlite‑vec k‑nearest‑neighbour search over OpenAI text‑embedding‑3‑small (API key) · reciprocal rank fusion, k = 60 · without embeddings: BM25 only, reported as degraded · 8 source adapters · figures found by caption, cropped (≤ 20 from the first 60 pages), described in ≤ 120 words, ≤ 200 a day by default

Deep Research, plan first

Deep Research drafts a plan of sub-questions and the sources for each. You can edit the plan; nothing runs until you press Start.

Method · sources: knowledge base, paper search, web search · quick / standard / deep: ≤ 3 / 6 / 10 sub‑questions, 0.4 / 1.2 / 3 M tokens (75 % for research workers) · stopped or out of budget: report written from the findings so far, marked partial · search, fetch and new‑paper caps listed in the report · versioned · Markdown zip or print to PDF
